What we store

Your game progress, score, settings and daily streak are stored only on your device. Fuse has no accounts, and we do not run a server that collects your personal information. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

Ads

Fuse shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can give a rough location), app interactions such as launches, taps and video views, and diagnostic information. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings, and manage ad personalisation there too. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Purchases, if offered, are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Notifications

If you choose to turn on a daily reminder, it is a notification created on your device. You can turn it off at any time in the game or in Android settings.

Children

Fuse is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
